PRIVACY POLICY

Edge Finder · last updated 15 November 2026

WHO THIS COVERS

Edge Finder is a personal tool run by the owner of The Vibe Game Dev on their own computer. It is not offered to the public, and it only signs in to the owner's own Google/YouTube and Instagram accounts.

WHAT IT ACCESSES

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos (the youtube.upload scope) to the owner's channel, and public YouTube data (search results, public video and channel statistics) through the YouTube Data API.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the owner's Instagram professional account.

HOW DATA IS STORED

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Public YouTube data is cached on that computer so it isn't requested again. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google, YouTube, Meta/Instagram and the AI services used to edit the owner's videos.

SHARING

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data about other people is collected beyond the public statistics YouTube shows to everyone.

REVOKING ACCESS

Access can be removed at any time from Google account permissions and from Instagram's settings (Apps and websites).

YOUTUBE API SERVICES

Edge Finder uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

CONTACT

Questions: open an issue at github.com/dexterelliot-code/edge-finder/issues.
