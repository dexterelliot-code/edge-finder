EDGE FINDER

A personal tool used by The Vibe Game Dev (@thevibegamedev), a channel about building real games with AI agents, in public.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Studies which YouTube Shorts in a niche outperform their channel's usual views, using public YouTube data.
  • Helps edit the channel owner's own game footage into Shorts, with narration and captions.
  • Uploads Shorts that the channel owner has reviewed and approved to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

WHO CAN USE IT

Edge Finder is not a public service. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and only ever signs in to the channel owner's own accounts.
